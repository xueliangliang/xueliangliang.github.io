Why?

This website was originally made for an assignment in my Master of Technology Management course, MT5003: Creativity and Innovation. After starting this project, I found it was a good opportunity to reflect on my life and ask myself these questions:


  • Who am I?
  • Where am I from?
  • What is my purpose of life?
  • Where am I going to be?

At age 35 in 2016, I was not able to answer all these questions yet. After completing MT5003, I continued this practice of reflection and persisted in pursuing my true purpose in life.
